Aftercare

Keep your hair
lasting longer.

Brushing

Brush gently morning and night. Support the weft with one hand while brushing to avoid pulling on the attachment area.

Sleeping

Always tie hair in a loose braid or low ponytail before bed. A silk or satin pillowcase helps reduce friction significantly.

Products

Use sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Keep oils and heavy products away from the attachment area to avoid slippage.

Swimming & exercise

Rinse with fresh water before and after swimming. After exercise, let hair dry fully before brushing. Tie back during workouts.

Heat

Always use a heat protectant spray before any styling. Extensions can be straightened and curled — just protect them first.

Move-ups

Schedule a move-up every 6–8 weeks as your hair grows out. Keeping up with this protects both your extensions and natural hair.
