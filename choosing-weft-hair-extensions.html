Choosing weft hair extensions: length, collection and weight

Choosing hair extensions starts with your natural hair, the result you hope to achieve and your maintenance preferences. Length and grams alone cannot determine whether a package is appropriate.

What length should I choose?

Hair Extensions Hub lists 18, 22 and 26-inch product lengths. The finished appearance varies with your starting hair length and how the extensions are blended.

What collections are available?

The published options are Regular Flat, Premium Mini and Luxury Flat Weft. A personal discussion helps narrow the choices based on density, colour, budget and the result you want.

Do I need 100g, 150g or 200g?

Those are the published package quantities, not automatic recommendations. A professional assessment is important, especially when your natural density is fine or uncertain. The online tool can compare packages but does not determine safe or suitable weight.
