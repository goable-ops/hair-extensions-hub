Choose the hair that suits you.

Hair is selected around your natural density, desired length, colour and the result you want. Available lengths include 18", 22" and 26". Choose from Regular Flat, Premium Mini and Luxury Flat Weft options, with full supplied-and-fitted package pricing shown above.

Premium

Premium Flat Weft

Flat, flexible double-drawn Remy hair designed to sit close to the scalp. A strong option for clients wanting natural volume and length without unnecessary bulk.

  • 18", 22" and 26" lengths
  • 50g, 100g, 150g and 200g plans depending on the client
  • Colour-match support before ordering
Premium & luxury

Mini / Genius Weft

A finer, discreet weft option for a seamless finish. Available in premium and luxury hair depending on softness, density, longevity and budget.

  • 18", 22" and 26" lengths
  • Amount tailored to your natural hair and goal
  • Installation quote confirmed with your hair selection
