Accessibility and contact

We aim to make Hair Extensions Hub easy to use for people with different access needs. We aim to follow WCAG 2.2 Level AA guidance; we do not claim verified full conformance.

Alternative ways to enquire

You do not need to use the online consultation or calculator to enquire. Message Donna directly to discuss services, prices, appointments or access needs. If you have difficulty with a form or page, tell us which page and what would help.

Using the online tools

The calculator and consultation guide are optional. Use your browser’s zoom settings to make text larger, or navigate between links and form controls with the Tab key. If a question is confusing, skip the online tools and tell Donna what outcome you want. You can ask for a written explanation of prices and maintenance before arranging an appointment.

Tell us about an access barrier

When reporting a problem, a page name and short description are enough. You do not need to share a diagnosis or medical details. Let us know if you prefer a different way to discuss the service or receive information.
