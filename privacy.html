Privacy and personal information

Who we are and how to contact us

Hair Extensions Hub provides mobile weft hair extension services in Moreton Bay, Queensland. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, complaints or marketing opt-out requests, contact Donna through our business Messenger. Please do not send identity documents, card details or sensitive health information through Messenger.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may receive your name, contact information, service preferences, appointment details, hair reference photos you choose to share, and relevant business communications. Our internal client records may also contain appointment history, prices, maintenance preferences and service notes. Payment details are handled separately through Square where used; this website does not ask for card details.

Consultation, calculator and appointment tools

The public tools prepare preliminary options, price estimates and editable requests in your browser. They do not automatically transmit your consultation answers or appointment request to us. If you choose to copy and send a summary through Messenger, that service processes the information under its own terms and privacy policy. If you choose our separate secure enquiry form, the details you submit, any pasted summary and applicable campaign labels are encrypted in your browser before being stored in the website enquiry inbox. Only our unlocked owner workspace can decrypt them. Cloudflare Turnstile helps reject automated submissions. Providing contact and service details is voluntary; without enough information we may not be able to assess your enquiry or arrange a service.

Use and disclosure

We use information to respond to enquiries, assess services, arrange and manage appointments, maintain business records, address complaints and comply with legal obligations. We may use service providers for website hosting, communication, bookings and payment processing, including Cloudflare, Meta/Messenger and Square. These providers may process information outside Australia; their processing locations depend on their services. We will not intentionally publish client records or use identifiable client photographs in marketing without appropriate permission.

Cookies, session storage and campaign attribution

The website uses browser session storage for non-personal campaign labels (for example, the referring platform and whether a campaign is paid or organic). These labels may appear in an editable summary if you use a campaign link. They are not automatically sent by the consultation tool. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics on the public website for aggregate traffic measurement. The private owner subdomain is excluded from this configuration. The site may also be subject to ordinary hosting and security logs; externally hosted fonts and linked services may receive standard connection information. This website does not include advertising pixels. We will update this notice before introducing new tracking technology.

Security and retention

We use restricted access and encryption for the private client workspace. No system is completely risk-free. We aim to retain records only for purposes for which they are required, including applicable legal and tax record-keeping obligations, and review records when those purposes end. Please contact us if you believe your information has been mishandled.

Marketing choices

Receiving a quote or giving us your contact details does not automatically subscribe you to marketing. We send promotional messages only where we have appropriate consent. You can withdraw consent by replying STOP or asking us through our business contact channel. We will respect your opt-out request and continue necessary service communications separately.

Access, correction and complaints

Contact us to request access to or correction of your information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need reasonable verification of your identity before disclosing records. We will review the concern and respond. If the Australian Privacy Act applies and you remain dissatisfied, you may seek guidance from the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
