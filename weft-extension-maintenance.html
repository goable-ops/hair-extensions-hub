How often do weft hair extensions need maintenance?

Hair Extensions Hub generally plans weft maintenance around every six to eight weeks, depending on individual hair growth, existing installation and home care. The precise interval should be confirmed with Donna.

Why do wefts need move-ups?

Natural hair grows over time. Professional maintenance helps the specialist review the existing installation, placement, blend and suitability before refitting the wefts.

What determines my maintenance price?

The amount of hair, number of rows and condition of the existing installation can affect what service is required. Published maintenance prices range from $200 for up to 100g to $350 for four rows.

When should I contact my specialist?

If you are uncomfortable, concerned about the condition of your hair or scalp, or uncertain whether your extensions are ready for maintenance, contact your specialist rather than relying on an online guide. This article is general information, not a diagnosis.
