# BizDr hair extension business framework
## Launch gates (Australia / Queensland pilot)
1. Confirm legal entity, registered trading name, ABN, GST status, public contact details, genuine service area, and licence/infection-control requirements for specific services and council. No personal residential address by default for a mobile operator; confirm an appropriate business contact.
2. Review consumer terms (final total price, cancellation/deposit policy, refunds and non-excludable Australian Consumer Law guarantees), verify testimonials and photo permissions. Confirm all price data and maintenance guidance.
3. Confirm Privacy Act coverage, privacy contact, actual provider locations and data processing terms, retention schedule, access/correction and complaint workflow, incident response, and secure backups. Do not assume overseas hosting is Australian-only. No client data in public demos. Consent for direct marketing separate from service communications; no imported consent without evidence. Record opt-outs and honour across channels.
4. Verify WCAG 2.2 AA against actual mobile/desktop, keyboard, screen readers, colour contrast, forms, focus, zoom, text alternatives. No conformance claim without testing.
5. Validate security headers, HTTPS, access controls, secrets storage, private owner separation, no client data leakage, backup restore and API failure behaviour. Exclude backend source from public build output; redirects are not an access boundary. Do not expose source, IDs or tokens unnecessarily.
6. Square remains authoritative for bookings/payments unless client chooses a different system. Verify read-only scoped OAuth and prevent duplicate records; no independent availability publication while Square is authoritative.
7. Website: local intent, unique title/meta, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots, semantic headings, service schema with verified data only, genuine reviews/photography, accessible crawlable guides. No fake locations or scale-generated duplicate pages. For AI search, publish useful question-led answers, direct service expertise, original photos and clear business identity; no guarantee of ranking.
8. Verify Google Search Console domain property via DNS with authorised owner; submit valid production sitemap only after live public pages allow indexing. Inspect homepage, service pages, guides, calculator and booking flow. Preview and owner portal must remain noindex. Maintain Google Business Profile service area, hours, business details and reviews; use exact real information.
9. Attribution: use non-personal campaign tags, distinguish organic/paid, link to consultations and bookings. Manual platform metrics until permitted integrations verified. Reconcile completed visits and actual revenue, exclude marketing opt-outs. Measure qualified enquiries, booked and completed visits, acquisition costs and margin.
10. Each future installation: separate deployment, data/credentials, client-specific pricing, brand, legal jurisdiction, consent records, accessibility tests, legitimate claims, and independent control. NZ/US require separate jurisdiction-specific legal review rather than copying AU policy text.
## Pilot status
- Public preview: policy/terms/accessibility pages in review, knowledge hub and three guides, metadata, service schema, sitemap prepared; noindex intentionally unchanged.
- Owner dashboard: encrypted vault, explicit marketing consent/opt-out and reminder draft gating; no auto marketing.
