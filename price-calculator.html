Questions before your appointment

What is included in a hair package?

Your selected hair and professional installation. Your precise colour match, suitability and final service are confirmed before ordering.

Can I supply my own hair?

Yes. The published BYO fresh weft installation price is $250, provided the hair is suitable.

How often will I need maintenance?

Most clients require move-ups around every 6–8 weeks, depending on growth, hair type and home care. The right schedule is personal, so confirm it with Donna.

What if I have fine hair or am unsure what is suitable?

Start with a personal assessment. The consultation guide does not automatically recommend weight or installation for fine or uncertain hair density.

How do I book?

Prepare an appointment request or message Donna. All appointments require confirmation.
